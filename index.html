Home›Sales & Purchase

Sales & Purchase Book

Upload the raw Sales and Purchase sheets — party summaries, details and the monthly VAT-return reconciliation generate automatically as a complete workbook.

Accepts one workbook containing both sheets, or two separate files. Expected columns: Date (B.S., e.g. 2081.04.01), Bill No., Party Name, Pan No., Tax Free, Taxable Amount, Vat. Embedded month-total rows are detected and stripped automatically.

Import summary
Column mapping
Tell the importer which column is which when a client's headers aren't standard. Date/Month, Party Name and Taxable Amount are required; the rest are optional.
Data Doctor
Every problem found in the uploaded book, each with a one-click fix. Corrections re-read the file from source, are logged, and travel with the workbook as a "Corrections" sheet.
Possible duplicate parties
Same party, different spellings? Tick the names that belong together, then apply. Unticked names stay separate — nothing merges without your approval.
Monthly reconciliation — As Per VAT Return
Type each month's figures from the filed VAT returns. "As per Book" is computed from the uploaded sheets; any difference of a rupee or more is flagged as a real gap (sub-rupee differences are the return's whole-rupee truncation). Typed figures autosave per client and fiscal year.
