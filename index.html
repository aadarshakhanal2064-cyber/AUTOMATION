Automation Hub›Autobooks

Autobooks

Upload the raw Sales and Purchase sheets — party summaries, details and the monthly VAT-return reconciliation generate automatically as a complete workbook.

Accepts one workbook containing both sheets, or two separate files. Expected columns: Date (a B.S. date like 2082.04.01 or just the month name), Bill No., Party Name, Pan No., Tax Free, Taxable Amount, Vat — plus, on the Purchase sheet only, Taxable Import, Import VAT, Capital Purchase and Capital VAT. Embedded month-total rows are detected and stripped automatically, and regenerated in the output. Not sure of the layout? Use Download data-entry format above.

Saved book
Saving stores every bill line, the party list and the import decisions for this client and fiscal year, so you can come back to the confirmations days later without re-uploading anything. A saved book reopens automatically when you pick the client and year again.
Import summary
Column mapping
Tell the importer which column is which when a client's headers aren't standard. Date/Month, Party Name and Taxable Amount are required; the rest are optional.
Data Doctor
Every problem found in the uploaded book, each with a one-click fix. Corrections re-read the file from source, are logged, and travel with the workbook as a "Corrections" sheet.
Possible duplicate parties
Same party, different spellings? Tick the names that belong together, then apply. Unticked names stay separate — nothing merges without your approval.
Monthly reconciliation — As Per VAT Return
The filed figures, month by month. Upload the VAT Return Detail sheet to fill them in, or type them by hand. "As per Book" is computed from the uploaded register; any difference of a rupee or more is flagged as a real gap (sub-rupee differences are the return's whole-rupee truncation). Figures autosave per client and fiscal year.
Sales / Purchase Register
The register as it reads on paper — bills in fiscal-month order with a total after each month. Omitted bills are appended after the Ashadh total, never folded into the month they belong to, and the Grand Total says how many it includes.
Record an omitted bill
A bill that wasn't available when the year's register was entered and closed. It is kept apart from the closed months, shows after the Ashadh total in the register, and still counts toward its party's total so the confirmation can reconcile. Pick the party from the register rather than retyping the name — the same party is often spelled differently on a late bill, and picking it is what keeps the two totals together.
Omitted bills on this book
Returns and debit notes carry the opposite sign, so the net effect below is what the register's Grand Total actually moves by.
Confirmation reconciliation
What each party confirmed, beside what the books say. Difference = Books − Confirmation: a negative figure means the books are short of what the party confirmed, which is usually a bill that surfaced after the register closed. Within Rs 1,000 counts as matched; beyond that it is reported as a difference. Every figure saves as you leave the field. The two tiers are separated because Annexure-13 reports them differently.
Annexure-13
One row per PAN, combining what was bought from and sold to that party — a party can be both. Only parties transacting Rs 1,00,000 or more on either side are listed, which is what the ≥/< 1 lakh tiering is for. Choose Goods or Service per side; the Capital / Others split is taken from the book's own Capital Purchase column rather than asked for, since that is a fact about the bill, not the party.
Reconciliation statements
Three year-end statements proving the filed returns and the books agree, and naming every reason they don't. Each runs from the filed figure to the books, so an adjustment is book − return. A residual below Rs 1,000 is absorbed as rounding; at or above that it stays on the face of the statement as a real unexplained difference. Adjustment lines are free text because which mistakes exist varies per client and per year.
